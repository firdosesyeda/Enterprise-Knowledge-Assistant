OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The onboarding process ensures new employees are productive, integrated, and
informed within their first 90 days. The process is divided into three phases:
Pre-Joining, Week 1, and the 30-60-90 Day Plan.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PHASE 1: PRE-JOINING (Before Day 1)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Responsibilities - HR Team:
□ Send offer letter and collect signed acceptance
□ Initiate background verification (typically 5-7 business days)
□ Send joining kit via email:
  - Welcome letter from CEO
  - First day logistics (time, location, parking)
  - List of documents to bring on Day 1
  - Pre-joining reading materials

Responsibilities - IT Team:
□ Provision laptop and accessories (standard: ThinkPad T14 or MacBook Pro 14)
□ Create corporate email account (firstname.lastname@techcorp.com)
□ Set up access to core tools: Slack, Jira, Confluence, GitHub/GitLab
□ Configure VPN credentials

Documents Required on Day 1:
- Aadhaar card (original + photocopy)
- PAN card (original + photocopy)
- Last 3 months' salary slips (from previous employer)
- Relieving letter from previous employer
- Education certificates (10th, 12th, Graduation, Post-Graduation)
- 4 passport-size photographs
- Bank account details for payroll setup

PHASE 2: WEEK 1 SCHEDULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Day 1 - Orientation:
09:00 AM - Welcome by HR, office tour
10:00 AM - IT setup: laptop, email, tools access
11:00 AM - Meet the team and direct manager
12:30 PM - Team lunch (company sponsored)
02:00 PM - HR Policy walkthrough (leave, attendance, benefits)
04:00 PM - Introduction to company culture and values
05:30 PM - Q&A session with HR

Day 2-3 - Department Onboarding:
- Deep-dive into department structure and objectives
- Meet cross-functional stakeholders
- Access to department-specific tools and codebases
- Review current projects and sprint backlog

Day 4-5 - Technical/Role Setup:
- Development environment setup (for engineers)
- Access to required systems (CRM, ERP, analytics tools)
- Review first assignment with manager
- Buddy assignment: each new hire gets a buddy from the team

PHASE 3: 30-60-90 DAY PLAN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

First 30 Days - LEARN:
Goals:
- Understand the product, codebase, and team processes
- Complete all mandatory compliance training (GDPR, Security, Code of Conduct)
- Shadow at least 3 team members across different functions
- Complete first small task or ticket

Mandatory Training Modules (must be completed within 30 days):
1. Information Security Awareness (online, ~2 hours)
2. GDPR and Data Privacy (online, ~1.5 hours)
3. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) (online + in-person, ~3 hours)
4. Code of Conduct and Ethics (online, ~1 hour)

Days 31-60 - CONTRIBUTE:
Goals:
- Independently complete assigned tasks
- Participate in sprint planning and retrospectives
- Provide feedback on onboarding experience to HR
- 30-day review with manager (informal check-in)

Days 61-90 - OWN:
Goals:
- Lead at least one small project or initiative
- Set 6-month goals with manager
- Complete 90-day performance review
- Transition from probation (if on 90-day probation period)

PROBATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All new employees serve a 90-day probation period.
During probation:
- Notice period is 2 weeks (not 1-2 months as for confirmed employees)
- Remote work is not permitted
- Performance is reviewed at 30, 60, and 90 days
- At day 90, the manager submits a confirmation recommendation to HR

Probation may be extended by up to 30 additional days if performance
is below expectations. A second extension is not permitted;
employees who do not meet expectations after the extended probation
will be separated from the company.
