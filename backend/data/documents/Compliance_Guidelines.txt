SECTION 1: DATA CLASSIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 Classification Levels
All company data must be classified into one of four levels:

PUBLIC
- Information approved for external release
- Examples: marketing materials, public website content, press releases
- Handling: No restrictions on sharing

INTERNAL
- Information intended for employees only
- Examples: internal announcements, process documents, meeting notes
- Handling: Do not share outside the organization without approval

CONFIDENTIAL
- Sensitive business information
- Examples: financial reports, client contracts, product roadmaps, HR records
- Handling: Share only on need-to-know basis; encryption required in transit

RESTRICTED
- Highest sensitivity; unauthorized disclosure causes severe damage
- Examples: source code, encryption keys, PII databases, board discussions
- Handling: Strict access control, audit logging, encryption at rest and in transit

1.2 Data Handling Rules by Classification
All employees must follow these rules when handling data:
- Label documents with their classification level
- Store confidential/restricted data only in approved systems
- Never store restricted data on personal devices
- Report suspected data breaches within 1 hour to security@techcorp.com

SECTION 2: GDPR AND PRIVACY COMPLIANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Personal Data Definition
Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
This includes: names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, location data,
biometric data, and any combination of data that can identify a person.

2.2 Employee Obligations
All employees who handle personal data must:
- Process data only for specified, legitimate purposes
- Obtain valid consent before collecting personal data from customers
- Respond to data subject access requests within 30 days
- Never transfer personal data to countries without adequate protection
- Delete personal data when it is no longer needed

2.3 Data Breach Response
In the event of a data breach:
1. Contain the breach immediately (disconnect systems if necessary)
2. Notify the Information Security team within 1 hour
3. Do not attempt to independently investigate or remediate
4. The Data Protection Officer (DPO) will assess and notify regulators within 72 hours if required
5. Document all actions taken

SECTION 3: ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Company Devices and Systems
Company-provided devices (laptops, phones, etc.) are for business use.
Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not:
- Violate any laws or regulations
- Consume excessive bandwidth or resources
- Expose the company to security risks

Prohibited activities on company systems:
- Installing unauthorized software
- Accessing illegal or inappropriate content
- Mining cryptocurrency
- Sharing login credentials with others
- Bypassing security controls or VPNs

3.2 Password Policy
All passwords must:
- Be at least 12 characters long
- Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Not be reused for the previous 12 passwords
- Be changed every 90 days for privileged accounts
- Never be written down or shared

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for:
- All email and communication accounts
- Cloud services (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- VPN access
- Admin/privileged accounts

3.3 Third-Party Software and AI Tools
Use of AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot) is permitted for productivity
but employees must NOT input:
- Customer personal data
- Source code marked Restricted
- Financial projections or unreleased product information
- Any Confidential or Restricted company information

Approved AI tools list is maintained at intranet.techcorp.internal/ai-tools.

SECTION 4: VENDOR AND THIRD-PARTY MANAGEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Vendor Onboarding Requirements
Before sharing company data with any vendor or third party:
- Legal must review and sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Security must conduct a vendor risk assessment (questionnaire + review)
- Approval required from the department head and CTO for Restricted data access

4.2 Vendor Access Termination
When a vendor contract ends:
- Revoke all access within 24 hours
- Request data deletion confirmation within 30 days
- Archive the vendor's DPA for 7 years

SECTION 5: PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE

- Level 1 (minor unintentional): Training and written warning
- Level 2 (repeated or negligent): Formal disciplinary action, potential suspension
- Level 3 (intentional or severe): Immediate termination and potential legal action
