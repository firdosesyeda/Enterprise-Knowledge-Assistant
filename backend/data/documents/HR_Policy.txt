SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 Annual Leave Entitlement
All permanent employees are eligible for 24 paid leaves annually.
This includes:
- 12 Casual Leaves (CL): For personal matters, can be taken 1-3 days at a time
- 12 Sick Leaves (SL): Requires medical certificate for more than 2 consecutive days
- National Holidays: 12 days per year as declared by the government

1.2 Leave Carry-Forward
Unused casual leaves may be carried forward up to a maximum of 6 days per year.
Sick leaves cannot be carried forward. All unused sick leaves lapse at year end.

1.3 Leave Application Process
- Apply at least 3 days in advance for planned leaves (except emergencies)
- Use the HRMS portal at portal.techcorp.internal to submit leave requests
- Manager approval required within 2 business days

1.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks for the first two children (as per Maternity Benefit Act 2017)
- Paternity Leave: 5 days, to be taken within 30 days of child's birth
- Adoption Leave: 12 weeks for the primary caregiver

SECTION 2: WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Standard Work Hours
- Core hours: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday
- Flexible start time: 8:00 AM to 10:30 AM
- Total working hours: 9 hours per day (including 1-hour lunch break)

2.2 Remote Work Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week subject to:
- Manager approval (must be pre-approved each week)
- Minimum 6 months tenure with the company
- Not applicable during probation period (first 90 days)
- Reliable internet connection and dedicated workspace required

2.3 Overtime
Overtime is compensated at 1.5x the hourly rate for hours beyond 9 per day.
Overtime must be pre-approved by the department head.

SECTION 3: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Salary
Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.
CTC (Cost to Company) structure:
- Basic Salary: 40% of CTC
- HRA (House Rent Allowance): 20% of CTC
- Special Allowance: 30% of CTC
- PF (Provident Fund): 12% of Basic (employer contribution)
- Medical Allowance: ₹15,000 per year

3.2 Performance Bonus
Annual performance bonuses are awarded based on individual and company performance:
- Outstanding (5): 20% of annual CTC
- Exceeds Expectations (4): 15% of annual CTC
- Meets Expectations (3): 10% of annual CTC
- Needs Improvement (2): No bonus
- Unsatisfactory (1): No bonus; PIP initiated

3.3 Health Insurance
All employees and their immediate family members are covered under group health insurance:
- Coverage: ₹5,00,000 per family per year
- Cashless hospitalization at 500+ network hospitals
- Pre-existing conditions covered after 12-month waiting period

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Professional Behavior
All employees must maintain professional conduct at all times.
Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited.
Violations will result in immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination.

4.2 Confidentiality
Employees must not share company information, client data, or trade secrets.
NDAs must be signed before access to sensitive projects is granted.
Data classification: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted.

SECTION 5: SEPARATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Notice Period
- Probation period employees: 2 weeks notice
- 0–2 years tenure: 1 month notice
- 2+ years tenure: 2 months notice
Buyout of notice period is possible with mutual agreement.

5.2 Exit Process
Employees must return all company assets (laptop, ID card, access badges).
Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of last working day.
Experience letters are issued within 15 days of separation.
