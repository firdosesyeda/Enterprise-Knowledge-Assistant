Q: What are the available pricing plans?
A: We offer three plans:
   - Starter: $10/user/month (up to 100 users, 1TB storage)
   - Business: $8/user/month (101–1,000 users, 10TB storage)
   - Enterprise: Custom pricing for 1,000+ users, unlimited storage
   Annual billing receives a 20% discount on all plans.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, and wire transfers for Enterprise accounts.
   All transactions are processed in USD.

Q: What is the refund policy?
A: Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the initial purchase (new accounts only).
   After 30 days, monthly subscriptions are not refundable for the current billing period.
   Annual subscriptions are eligible for a prorated refund for unused full months.
   To request a refund, contact billing@techcorp.com with your account ID and reason.

Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

Q: What file types does CloudSync Pro support?
A: CloudSync Pro supports all file types up to 5GB per file.
   Special handling for: Office documents (auto-preview), PDF (OCR search), Images (thumbnail generation).

Q: Is there a storage limit?
A: Starter: 1TB shared across all users.
   Business: 10TB shared across all users.
   Enterprise: Unlimited storage (subject to fair use policy).

Q: What is the maximum file size for upload?
A: 5GB per individual file. For files larger than 5GB, use the desktop client's chunked upload feature.

Q: Does CloudSync Pro support real-time collaboration?
A: Yes. Multiple users can edit documents simultaneously. Conflict resolution uses operational transforms.
   Real-time cursors and presence indicators are shown in the Business and Enterprise plans.

SECTION 3: SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: How is my data protected?
A: All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
   We are SOC 2 Type II certified, ISO 27001 compliant, and GDPR ready.
   Data centers are located in India (Mumbai, Chennai) and Singapore.

Q: Can I control user access?
A: Yes. Role-based access control (RBAC) is available on all plans:
   - Viewer: Read-only access
   - Editor: Can edit and comment
   - Admin: Full access including user management
   - Owner: Billing and plan management

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: By default, data is stored in our Indian data centers. Enterprise customers can choose their region.

Q: What support options are available?
A: - Starter: Email support only (48-hour response SLA)
   - Business: Email + chat support (8-hour response SLA, Mon-Fri 9AM-6PM IST)
   - Enterprise: 24/7 phone, email, and dedicated account manager (1-hour SLA)

Q: How do I report a bug?
A: Submit bugs through support.techcorp.com or email bugs@techcorp.com.
   Include steps to reproduce, screenshots, and your browser/OS version.

Q: Is there a service level agreement (SLA)?
A: Yes. We guarantee 99.9% uptime for Business and Enterprise plans.
   Starter plan is best-effort with no formal SLA.
   Downtime compensation: 10% credit per hour of downtime beyond SLA.
